Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Nest & Nurture is a family organization application operated as an independent personal project. This policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Who Can Use the App

Nest & Nurture is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Children are not permitted to create accounts or independently use the application. Parents and caregivers may choose to enter information about children they care for and are responsible for ensuring they have the authority to provide that information.

2. Information You May Provide

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account information, such as your email address.
  • Family and child names and information you enter into trackers.
  • Calendar events, appointments, and reminders.
  • Meal plans and grocery lists.
  • Budget entries and transactions.
  • Tasks, checklists, and personal notes.
  • Messages or information you provide when contacting support.

Please avoid entering information you do not want stored in the application.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the application's features, save and retrieve your information, maintain and troubleshoot the service, respond to support requests, and help protect the application and its users.

4. Storage and Service Providers

Nest & Nurture uses third-party services to operate the application. These currently include:

  • Supabase: used for account authentication and cloud data storage.
  • GitHub Pages: used to host the application website.
  • Browser storage: some information may also be stored locally in your device's browser.

These providers may process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies. The services used by the app may change, and this policy will be updated when appropriate.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application or disclosed when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to address security issues.

6. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information, including using third-party authentication and storage services. However, no website, application, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and using a secure device.

7. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

Signing out, deleting a browser shortcut, or clearing browser storage does not necessarily delete information stored in your cloud account. Contact us to request deletion of cloud-stored account information.

8. Children's Information

The application is designed for adults, not for children to use directly. Information about children may be entered by a parent or caregiver for family organization purposes. Please contact us if you believe a child has independently created an account or provided information directly.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
