Privacy Policy

Nest & Nurture

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. About This Policy

Nest & Nurture is a family organization tool operated as an independent personal project. This policy explains how information is handled when adults use the application.

2. Who May Use the App

Nest & Nurture is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Children may not create accounts or independently use the application. Parents and caregivers may enter information about children they are authorized to care for and are responsible for the information they choose to enter.

3. Information You May Enter

Depending on the features you use, information may include account and email information, family member names, child and milestone information, calendar events, appointments, meal plans, grocery lists, budgets, transactions, tasks, checklists, and notes.

Please avoid entering information that is not necessary to use the app, especially highly sensitive personal information.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the app's features, save and retrieve your information, maintain accounts, troubleshoot problems, respond to support requests, and help protect the service.

5. Service Providers and Storage

The app uses third-party services, including GitHub Pages for website hosting and Supabase for account authentication and cloud data services. Information may be processed or stored by these providers as necessary to operate the app and is subject to their respective privacy policies and practices.

The app may also use storage in your browser. Information stored only in your browser may not be available on another device.

6. Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the app, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or others.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to help protect information. However, no website, online service, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

Signing out or removing the app from your device does not necessarily delete information stored in your account or with service providers.

9. Children's Information

The app is designed for adult users, not for children to use independently. Parents and caregivers who enter information about children are responsible for ensuring they have the authority to do so.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
